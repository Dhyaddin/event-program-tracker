About This Event

The AI & Machine Learning Summit 2025 is UPM's premier annual technology conference bringing together students, researchers, and industry professionals to explore the latest advancements in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

This full-day event features keynote speeches from leading AI researchers, hands-on technical workshops, research paper presentations, and networking sessions. Whether you're a beginner curious about AI or an advanced researcher in the field, this summit has something for everyone.

What you will learn:
  • Latest trends in deep learning and neural networks
  • Practical AI applications in healthcare, finance, and agriculture
  • Introduction to Large Language Models (LLMs)
  • AI ethics and responsible AI development
  • How to start your career in AI research
